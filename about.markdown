Hi! This website is a passion project alongside my 9-5 focus on quantum spins to both collate and share work I'm most proud of. I enjoy travelling, photography, and -- I discovered since starting this project -- website design. I take photos from both a Canon compact and my Samsung S22. My background is in landscape/nature shots, but I have also been trying to expand into urban shots and still life. I am a big fan of the <em>spontaneity</em> of scenes -- when something you pass by perks your interest and you decide to capture it by means of a photo rather than just pass by and let it go. Hence my Samsung S22!
